# 0005 APIs: lookup, search, entity, reconciliation, calendars

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-09

## Decision

All read-only, JSON with CORS, documented on `/api/`:

- **Lookup** `/api/lookup?id=…` and **resolve** `/api/resolve?id=…` (303): the confref identifiers for an identifier of
  a source (dblp keys and IRIs of events, venues and publications; Wikidata; GND; ConfIDent and portal pages;
  Springer IRIs), of proceedings (DOI, ISBN) or a former confref identifier. Keys are normalised (case, scheme,
  trailing slash, DOI and ISBN forms); the numbered parts of a dblp event are also found by the key without the number.
- **Search** `/api/search` and **entity** `/api/entity/<id>`.
- **Reconciliation** `/reconcile`: the W3C reconciliation API 0.2 as OpenRefine uses it (manifest, queries by GET
  and POST, suggest, preview); types `event` and `series`; properties `year`, `country`, `acronym`. A score of 85 or
  more with a lead of 10 over the next candidate is a match.
- **Calendars** `/feeds/upcoming.ics`, `/feeds/series/<id>.ics`, `/feeds/field/<code>.ics`: events from today.
- **Status** `/status.json`: the versions loaded and the figures of the dashboard.
